About this trek

Follow the historic cattlemen's trails through Victoria's high plains — the same routes made famous in "The Man from Snowy River." Four days of riding through eucalypt forest, alpine grassland and river valleys, camping under the stars each night with a fully supported pack team handling gear, meals and horse care. No prior overnight riding experience needed.

Day-by-day itinerary

1Day
Base camp to Bluff River

Meet at base camp for horse allocation and a safety briefing, then ride 14km through open grassland to our first riverside camp.

🐎 14km⛰️ Easy–moderate
2Day
Bluff River to High Plains

Climb onto the high plains proper — the longest and most spectacular day, with sweeping views across the Alpine National Park.

🐎 22km⛰️ Moderate
3Day
Rest day & ridge exploration

Optional half-day ride to Mt Feathertop lookout, or rest at camp. Campfire dinner with the guiding team.

🐎 Optional 10km⛰️ Easy
4Day
Return to base camp

Ride back down through the valley to base camp, arriving early afternoon in time for departure.

🐎 16km⛰️ Easy–moderate
