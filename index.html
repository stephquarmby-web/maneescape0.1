What we are

The equestrian travel marketplace

Mane Escape is a discovery platform for riders — not a tour operator. We help you find quality operators, rural stays, and unforgettable rides in regions where horse travel has always been part of the landscape.

Bring your own horse

Horse owners travelling with their horse need spelling yards, paddocks, trail access, and agistment-friendly accommodation at the destination.

Guided experiences

From a half-day trail ride to a multi-day supported trek — find guided equestrian experiences with or without your own horse.

Browse by type

Four ways to ride & stay

Every listing on Mane Escape fits one of four experience types — each with its own character and what riders need to know upfront.

Day Rides

Guided trail rides from half-day to full day. Operator-supplied horses, group sizes, and experience levels.

Multi-day Rides & Treks

Overnight guided treks with camping or accommodation. Day-by-day itineraries and upcoming departures.

Horse-Friendly Accommodation

Properties that welcome horses on arrival — stables, yards, paddocks, and riding access priced separately.

Holiday with Your Horse

BYO horse stays with your horse bundled into the nightly rate. Trail access, requirements, and rural hospitality.
