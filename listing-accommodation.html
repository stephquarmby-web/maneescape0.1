About this property

A working farmstay in the Thredbo Valley with direct access to Kosciuszko National Park trails. Yards, stables and paddocks are available for horses on arrival, priced separately from your human accommodation — pick exactly what you need for your stay. Ten minutes from Jindabyne, with guided trail rides available on-property or self-guided access to the national park network.
