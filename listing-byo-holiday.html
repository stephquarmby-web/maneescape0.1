About this retreat

Bring your own horse to this coastal property at the base of Golden Bay's beach trails. Your horse stays right alongside you — paddocks and stables are steps from your cottage — with direct self-guided beach access and optional guided rides into the surrounding hill country. Horse accommodation is bundled into your nightly rate, so there's one simple price for you and your horse.
