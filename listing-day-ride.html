About this ride

Climb out of Jindabyne along the alpine ridgeline for sweeping views across the Snowy Mountains high country. Our sure-footed mountain horses are matched to your experience level, and small group sizes mean plenty of one-on-one time with your guide. Suitable for beginners, with an optional canter section for more confident riders.

Experience levels

Beginner

Walk-pace throughout, full instruction provided. No prior riding needed.

Intermediate

Comfortable at walk and trot independently. Some trail experience helpful.

Advanced

Confident at canter on varied terrain. Optional faster-paced group.
